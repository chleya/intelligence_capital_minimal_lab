# Intelligence Capital Theory — Lab Summary

## Core Thesis

Intelligence is world-change information that has been compressed, throttled, structurally retained, and then realized as value in new contexts.

## Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Change Capital | Effective information about how the world changes, not what it is |
| Throttling | Selective retention of change events under capacity constraints |
| Intelligence Appreciation | Throttled capital generates more value in a new context than storage cost |
| Operational Realization | Throttled capital produces actual downstream value, not just predicted value |
| Bad Debt / False Capital | Structure that appears to be capital but cannot transfer, realize, or is shortcut-explained |
| IAR | Intelligence Appreciation Rate = [V(S, C_new) - V(baseline, C_new)] / Cost(S) |
| ORR | Operational Realization Rate = control_dividend / prediction_dividend |
| BDR | Bad Debt Ratio = shortcut_explainable_gain / total_claimed_gain |

## Relation to AEP

AEP (Action-Effect Profile) is one specific form of change capital — action-effect capital. The AEP program demonstrated that action-effect prediction capital can be accumulated but cannot necessarily be realized as control capital. This realization gap is the central research problem.
